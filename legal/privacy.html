1. Who we are

Walking Companion is an iPhone app that coaches incline treadmill walking and jogging workouts. It is made and published by the Walking Companion developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the Walking Companion app. It applies to the app and to this website.

Because Walking Companion keeps your information only on your own device, we do not receive, store or process your personal data. In data-protection terms, we are not a controller of anything you enter into the app.

2. What the app keeps on your iPhone

To build your plan and show your progress, Walking Companion saves the following in its private storage on your iPhone. Other apps cannot read it, and we cannot see it.

Your profile
First name or nickname (optional), age, sex (or “prefer not to say”), height, starting and goal weight, goal, pace, daily activity level and units.
Health check answers
Your yes-or-no answers to the readiness questions (for example, whether a doctor has told you that you have a heart condition, or whether you are pregnant). They are used only to set safe starting levels for your plan.
Your plan and settings
Training days, session length, treadmill limits, paces, voice and cue preferences, reminder time and appearance.
Workout history
Date, duration, intervals, speeds, inclines, distance, climb, estimated active energy, effort ratings and any notes you write.
Weight and measurements
The weigh-ins and measurements you log. If you connect Apple Health, weights recorded there are read from Apple Health each time you open the app; they are not stored by Walking Companion or included in backups.
Progress
Badges earned, lessons read and the date of your last backup.

Energy figures in the app are estimates calculated on your iPhone from published exercise equations. Nothing is calculated on a server.

3. What we never do

  • We do not run servers for the app, and the app makes no network requests. It works fully in airplane mode.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or social-media software in the app.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell, rent, share or trade any information, because we never receive any.
  • We do not send your information to artificial-intelligence services. Walking Companion’s Quick Draft workouts are generated on your iPhone.
  • We do not sync your data to iCloud or any other cloud service.

4. Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional and off until you turn it on. Walking Companion asks for permission only after you tap Connect Apple Health during setup or in Me → Apple Health. The app works fully if you never connect it or if you decline.

What Walking Companion can save to Apple Health

  • Each finished treadmill session as an indoor walking or running workout, with its time, estimated distance, estimated active energy, climb and average effort (METs). If you tell Walking Companion you also record on Apple Watch, it saves the workout without energy and distance so they are not counted twice.
  • The weights you log in Walking Companion, as body-mass entries.

What Walking Companion can read from Apple Health

  • Body weight recorded by other apps and devices (such as a smart scale), so your weight trend stays up to date. Walking Companion reads nothing else.

How Apple Health data is used

Data read from Apple Health is used only inside Walking Companion to show your weight trend and progress. It is never sent off your iPhone, never shared with anyone, never sold, never used for advertising or marketing, and never stored by us in iCloud.

You can change or withdraw permission at any time in the iPhone Settings app → Health → Data Access & Devices → Walking Companion. Workouts and weights already saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

5. Notifications and voice cues

If you allow notifications, Walking Companion schedules them on your iPhone: training reminders on the days and time you choose, and interval-change alerts during a workout in case your screen locks. They are local notifications created by the app itself. No push service or server is involved, and no information leaves your device. You can turn them off in Settings → Notifications → Walking Companion.

Voice cues are spoken by your iPhone’s built-in speech voices. Walking Companion does not use the microphone, camera, location or contacts.

6. Backups and new phones

If you have turned on iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, your iPhone may include Walking Companion’s data in that device backup, the same as for other apps. That backup is created and protected by Apple according to your own settings; we have no access to it.

You can also save your own backup file at any time (see the next section) and use Restore from a backup to move your history to a new iPhone.

7. Export and erase your data

  • Export: in Me → Back up & restore, tap Export backup. Walking Companion creates a file and opens the share sheet, so you decide where it goes (for example Files, AirDrop or Mail). Once you share a file, the service you choose handles it under its own privacy terms.
  • Erase: in Me → Back up & restore, choose Erase all data to delete your profile, plan, history and weights from the app. Deleting the app also deletes its data from your iPhone.
  • Apple Health: erasing Walking Companion data does not delete what was already saved to Apple Health. Manage that in the Health app.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, and to object to its use. Because all of your Walking Companion data is held only on your iPhone, you can exercise these rights directly in the app: view and edit everything in the app, export it, or erase it. We hold no copy that we could give you or delete for you. If you have a question about these rights, contact us using the details below. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Children

Walking Companion is for ages 13 and up. Weight-loss targets and calorie intake suggestions are shown to adults only. It is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age for consent to data processing in your country. Because the app does not collect personal information from anyone, we hold no information about children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and describe important changes in the app’s release notes. If a future version ever needs to send any information off your iPhone (for example, an optional sync feature), we will explain it clearly and ask for your permission in the app before anything is sent.
