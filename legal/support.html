Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Hillpace keeps everything on your iPhone, so you can start training as soon as you finish the short setup.

Does Hillpace cost anything?

Hillpace is free to download and use, with no ads, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

Why do the voice cues stop when my screen locks?

iPhone pauses apps when the screen locks, so voice cues need the screen on. Hillpace keeps your screen awake during a workout, so leave the phone unlocked on the treadmill console.

As a safety net, allow lock-screen cues when Hillpace offers them before a workout. If the phone does lock, each interval change then arrives as a notification with a vibration, and with a sound when the Ring/Silent switch is set to ring. If you missed the prompt, turn notifications on in Settings → Notifications → Hillpace. If you use a Focus, allow Hillpace or time-sensitive notifications in that Focus.

I can’t hear the voice cues
  • Check that voice cues are on in Me → Voice & cues.
  • Turn the volume up with the side buttons while a workout is running.
  • If you use Bluetooth headphones, make sure they are connected before you start.
  • Beeps and the on-screen countdown keep guiding you even with voice cues off.
How accurate are the calorie numbers?

They are estimates. Hillpace uses the American College of Sports Medicine walking and running equations with your weight, speed and incline, and shows net active energy (the energy above what you would use at rest). Real energy use varies from person to person, so the figures can differ substantially from your actual burn and from treadmill or watch readings.

The full method is in Me → How we calculate. If you would rather not see calorie or weight numbers at all, you can train without them.

My treadmill shows a different distance

Treadmill belts are often calibrated a little differently. After a workout, open Match your treadmill on the summary and enter the time and distance from your console. Hillpace updates that workout, and its climb and energy estimate scale with it.

My treadmill has no incline, or a low maximum. Can I still use Hillpace?

Yes. During setup you tell Hillpace your treadmill’s maximum speed and incline, and every workout stays within them. On a treadmill without incline, climbs become faster walking intervals instead. You can change these limits in Me → Treadmill.

How do I connect Apple Health, and what does it save?

Go to Me → Apple Health and tap Connect Apple Health. Hillpace can save each finished workout as an indoor walking or running workout (time, estimated distance, estimated active energy and climb) and the weights you log. It reads body weight from other apps and scales to keep your trend up to date.

If you also record the session on Apple Watch, turn on the Apple Watch option in Me → Apple Health. Hillpace then saves the workout without energy and distance, so nothing is counted twice.

To change what Hillpace may read or write, open Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Hillpace.

How do training reminders work?

Turn them on in Me → Reminders and pick a time. Hillpace reminds you on your training days. Reminders are created on your iPhone and work without an internet connection.

I missed a session. What happens to my plan?

Nothing breaks. Open the session in the Plan tab to move it to today, skip it, or switch to the shorter busy-day version. Your effort ratings after each workout let Hillpace hold steady, ease off or offer a step up the following week.

How do I back up my data or move to a new iPhone?

Your data lives only on your iPhone, so keep a backup. In Me → Back up & restore, tap Export backup and save the file to Files, iCloud Drive or a computer. On a new iPhone, install Hillpace and tap Restore from a backup on the first screen, then choose that file.

A normal iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone usually includes Hillpace too.

How do I delete my data?

In Me → Back up & restore, choose Erase all data. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone. Anything already saved to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.

Is Hillpace a medical device?

No. Hillpace is a fitness coach, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to your doctor before starting a new exercise plan, especially if you answered yes to any setup health question.

Stop exercising and seek help if you feel chest pain or pressure, unusual breathlessness, dizziness, palpitations or nausea. The run screen has an I feel unwell button on the pause screen, and the stop signs are always in Me → Health & safety.
